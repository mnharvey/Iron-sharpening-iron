## About This Repository

This site documents my sermon preparation journey. Each sermon represents:
- Wrestling with Scripture
- Working through theology
- Processing personal experience
- Iron sharpening iron with AI tools, prayer, and community

**My approach:** "I think my approach is bearing my soul out to hopefully help others as I try to be better."

**Biblical foundation:** *"Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves receive from God."* - 2 Corinthians 1:3-4

## Technical Details

**Built with:**
- HTML/CSS for presentation
- GitHub Pages for hosting
- Claude AI for iron-sharpening-iron sermon development
- Prayer, Scripture study, and community feedback

**Sermon preparation workflow:**
1. Personal wrestling with topic/Scripture
2. Research (commentaries, original languages, cultural context)
3. Draft with AI assistance (Claude)
4. Edit for personal voice
5. Practice with pause marks
6. Preach and iterate

**Files structure:**
- `index.html` - Homepage
- `sermon_*.html` - Individual sermon files (web-optimized with print functionality)
- `sermon_*.docx` - Editable Word documents
- Pause marks `[PAUSE]` and `[LONG PAUSE]` visible on screen, hidden when printed
