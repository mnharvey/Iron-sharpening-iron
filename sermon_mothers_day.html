POINT 1: The Problem - When You're Drowning, Everything Feels Like a Sign

When you're in crisis mode, everything starts to look like a sign from God. The spam call feels like an opportunity. The loan offer feels like provision. The anxiety feels like a warning. The urgency feels like confirmation. The chaos feels permanent.

And you're asking: "God, is this YOU? Or is this just me panicking?"

The Parable of the Talents Makes It Harder, Matthew 25.

The master gives three servants money. Two invest it, double it - the master celebrates them. One buries it in the ground, plays it safe, returns exactly what he was given. The master calls him "wicked and lazy" and throws him out.

Here's the problem for moms: When you read that parable, it can souns like every parenting expert screaming at you:

"Don't be lazy - DO MORE"

"Don't waste your talents - BE MORE"

"Don't play it safe - ACHIEVE MORE"

"The servant who buried his talent was PUNISHED - so HUSTLE HARDER"

So moms may start thinking: Am I being the lazy servant if I'm not doing enough activities with my kids? Am I wasting my talents if I'm "just" at home?

And then - because you've been THINKING about it: Pinterest starts showing you perfect DIY projects. Instagram pushes posts about "mom wins." Facebook shows ads for classes, activities, opportunities.

And it feels like confirmation. "See? I SHOULD be doing more!"

But is it? I'm trying to put God at the helm. But is social media being evil? (Google removed "Don't be evil" from their employee handbook years ago. That should probably tell us something, It use to be on the first page)

Is this God calling you to be faithful with your talents? Or is this just algorithms exploiting your anxiety - feeding you ads based on your search history and calling it "conviction"?

Because the noise doesn't just come from the world. Sometimes it comes from Scripture itself - twisted by human (man made) culture, amplified by algorithms, weaponized by comparison.

And you can't tell the difference anymore between: God's voice calling you to faithful motherhood, and Pinterest using Jesus' parable to sell you a craft subscription.

How do you hear God's voice when even His parables get hijacked by the noise?

POINT 4: Be Still (The Command We Ignore)

Here's the verse we all should know but none of us obey because of our human nature:

Psalm 46:10

"Be still, and know that I am God."

It Sounds nice, Comforting. Peaceful. And God COMMANDS it.

The Hebrew word for "be still" is raphah. Phonetically: RAH-fah (like "rah" as in "rah rah" cheerleading, then "fah" as in "do-re-mi-fa")

It means: Let go. Release. Stop striving. Cease your frantic activity.

God is saying: STOP. [PAUSE] Not "figure it out faster." Not "make a decision NOW." but STOP. Be still. [PAUSE] Know that I am God. [LONG PAUSE]

You know why moms don't do it? From what I see, it's because being still feels like failing. When the kids need you, stopping feels irresponsible. When the house is a mess, pausing feels lazy. When everyone's counting on you, resting feels selfish.

But God says: Be still.

And Deuteronomy 6:5 reminds us WHY: "Love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength."

Are you seeking God with your WHOLE heart? Or just looking for Him to confirm what you already want to do?

Even in combat, experienced soldiers know: sometimes you have to take a knee. Not because the fight is over. But because rushing without assessing gets you killed.

"Slow is smooth, smooth is fast" - that's what they teach you. Take a knee. Observe. Orient. Decide. THEN act.

Because panic gets people killed. Stillness saves lives. You can't see the forest for the trees when you're just reacting to everything in front of you. You have to step back. Observe. See the whole situation.

Same for moms. When you're thrashing in the water, you drown faster. When you stop thrashing and let yourself float - you don't sink. You have to be still to hear God. You have to take a knee to see what He's actually doing.

The stillness will come. Not before the prayer. THROUGH the prayer.

POINT 5: How to Actually Discern God's Voice

Okay, so how do you actually DO this? How do you hear God when everything feels urgent? Here's a practical framework:

1. STOP (Psalm 46:10)

Before you make any decision, STOP. Get still. Breathe. Pray. Don't decide in the chaos. Decide in the stillness. (Or decide AFTER working through the chaos WITH God.)

2. CHECK THE FRUIT (Matthew 7:16)

Does this decision bring PEACE or ANXIETY? Colossians 3:15 says: "Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts." If the decision creates panic, it's probably not God. God's voice brings peace, not anxiety.

3. TEST IT AGAINST SCRIPTURE (2 Timothy 3:16)

Does this align with God's Word? Does it lead you into something God's already said NO to? Does it require you to compromise your integrity? God will never lead you to violate His Word.

4. SEEK GODLY COUNSEL (Proverbs 15:22)

"Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed." Talk to your spouse. Talk to a trusted friend. Talk to a mentor. Don't make big decisions in isolation. If you can't tell people about it, you probably shouldn't do it.

5. WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION (Isaiah 30:18)

"The LORD longs to be gracious to you; therefore he will rise up to show you compassion. For the LORD is a God of justice. Blessed are all who wait for him!"

If it's God, He'll confirm it multiple ways. If it's just noise, it'll fade. One Instagram post isn't confirmation. One Pinterest idea isn't a sign. One feeling isn't enough. Wait for God to confirm it through Scripture, through counsel, through peace.

6. ASK: DOES THIS BRING GLORY TO GOD? (1 Corinthians 10:31)

"Whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God." Am I doing this to build God's kingdom or mine? Am I seeking God's will or justifying my own? Am I making this decision out of faith or out of fear of comparison?

POINT 6: God's Voice vs. Noise

So how do you tell the difference?

God's Voice:

Brings PEACE, not panic

Aligns with SCRIPTURE

Is confirmed by GODLY COUNSEL

Doesn't RUSH you

Produces FRUIT (love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control - Galatians 5:22-23)

Brings CLARITY over time

Noise (social media, comparison, anxiety, algorithms):

Creates URGENCY

Produces ANXIETY

Contradicts SCRIPTURE

Isolates you from COUNSEL

Pushes you to decide NOW

Feels like CHAOS

Comes through your search history

Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)

Makes you feel behind

Amplifies comparison

God's voice doesn't come through Instagram ads. God's voice doesn't create panic about whether you're doing enough. God's voice doesn't push urgency based on what other moms are posting.
