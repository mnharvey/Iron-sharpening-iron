About This Journey

I'm a retired Army Major (25 years, Combat Engineer), veteran of Ramadi 2005-2006, and lay preacher at Mountain View Community Church. This site documents my sermon journey - not preaching FROM arrival, but THROUGH the process of learning to hear God's voice and share it with others.

Each sermon here represents iron sharpening iron - working through Scripture, theology, and personal experience. I'm bearing my soul to help others as I try to be better.
